## Adding or changing a member

One row per person, in a pull request to this repo, placed by first commit. `Name` is
`Family, Given`, as Zenodo writes it; `ORCID` and `GitHub` are links, or empty; `First commit` is
the commit's UTC date linking to it, or empty. Change `.zenodo.json` in
[marola-dev/marola](https://github.com/marola-dev/marola) in step, so the next release credits the
same people, and bump `site/members.version` in marola-site so the page shows the change.
