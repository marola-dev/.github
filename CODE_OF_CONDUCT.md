# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include: being respectful of
differing opinions, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is
best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include: sexualized language or imagery, trolling and insulting
comments, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without
permission, and other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities and scope

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and are expected to take appropriate
corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior. This applies in every marola-dev space
(issues, PRs, discussions) and when an individual is officially representing the project
elsewhere.

## Enforcement

There is no conduct email. Report an incident privately to a maintainer of the
[marola-dev](https://github.com/marola-dev) organization through GitHub; don't open a public
issue. Every complaint is reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
